# Simulated Company Intake Contract

Use this when the production company inquiry form is unavailable.

Required contextual fields supplied by the product:
- `source_type`: `student` or `project`
- `source_id`: e.g. `S01` or `P04`
- `source_name`
- `source_url`

Then collect from the employer:
- company name
- contact name
- contact email
- inquiry description

No employer account, CRM, or internal messaging system is required for Release 1.
